# Work through the steps

**EmbedRisk: Open guided wizard** walks the methodology from left to right:

1. Kickoff: define the device boundary
2. Assumptions: a living artifact
3. Impact workshop: worst case first
4. Components and assets: connect impact to CIA + Safety
5. Data flow diagram: where attack paths emerge
6. Threat analysis: rate and link to impact
7. Attack trees, mitigations and requirements
8. Review: validate and revisit

A TRA is never truly finished. Return to any step when the design, assumptions or threat landscape change.
